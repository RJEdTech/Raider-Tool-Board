# Raider Tool Bingo

A 4×4 card of classroom moves with the tools Regis Jesuit licenses. Tap a square when you've done it. Four in a row or a column wins, and you claim your badge in Canvas.

Live: https://rjedtech.github.io/Raider-Tool-Board/

It's embedded in the **Raider Tool Board** page of the faculty PD course in Canvas. School numbers, the Department Cup and the colleagues to ask live there, behind the Canvas login.

- `index.html`: the whole card. The `RTB_CONFIG` block at the top holds the squares and the claim-survey link.
- Teachers pick their subject first, so math, science, English and language teachers get squares for their own tools.
- Marks are saved in the browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere.
