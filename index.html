How to play

  1. Pick your subject, then try moves from the card.Math, science, English and language teachers get squares for their own tools. Each square links to a short how-to.
  2. Claim your badge in Canvas.Got four in a row or a column? Use the claim button on the card. It takes a minute.
  3. Get a shout-out.Badge holders are named in the monthly AI-in-Classroom brief and get a chance at Three Minutes of Fame at a faculty meeting.

Stuck on a square? Ask a colleague who already uses the tool. The Ask a Colleague list is on the Raider Tool Board in the faculty PD course.

School numbers, department winners and colleagues to ask are on the Raider Tool Board in Canvas (faculty login). Questions: Jason Beyer, Educational Technology.
